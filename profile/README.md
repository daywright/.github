# Daywright

**One brain you talk to, and open devices that see, count and tell you what to do
next.** Health first: getting busy people who were once in shape back in shape,
without holding a phone or having to think about it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Daywright Kitchen** | A voice scale. Say "one big tomato" and it is weighed and logged, hands-free. |
| **Daywright Board** | An e-ink screen on the fridge with today's plan. |
| **Daywright Pendant** | A wearable that sees meals and speaks quietly in your ear. |
| **The brain** | One timeline of your data, rules you say once, a daily plan. Self-host it, or use Daywright Cloud. |

## Status

Early, and built in public. Nothing ships yet; the
[roadmap](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md) says
what comes next and the
[decision log](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/blob/main/docs/decisions.md)
says why.

## Join the wrights

A wright is a maker. Daywright is built by people who write the software and design
the hardware together.

- Ideas and questions: [Discussions](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/discussions)
- Something to pick up: [good first issues](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22)
  and [help wanted](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22help+wanted%22)
- How to contribute: [CONTRIBUTING](https://github.com/daywright/daywright/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
  Sign off your commits; there is no contributor license agreement.
