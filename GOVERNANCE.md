# Governance

Daywright is young, so its governance is simple, and it is written down so it can
grow.

## Roles

- **Project lead:** @Ahmad-b1995 sets the direction, merges changes and has the
  final say until there is a maintainer team.
- **Maintainers:** contributors with a record of good changes and reviews are
  invited to maintain an area: the brain, the apps, or a device. Maintainers
  review and merge in their area and act as Community Moderators under the code of
  conduct.
- **Wrights:** everyone who contributes code, hardware, docs, testing or help.

## How decisions are made

- **Small changes:** a pull request and one maintainer's review.
- **Large changes** (a new device, a protocol change, anything that undoes an
  earlier decision): start in Discussions, then open an issue labelled `rfc`, and
  land the outcome as a decision record in the repository's `docs/decisions.md`.
- **The licenses do not change.** Contributions stay under the license of the
  folder they touch, and there is no contributor license agreement, so nobody can
  relicense the project from under its contributors.

## Conduct

The [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies in every Daywright space.
